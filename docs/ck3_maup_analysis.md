# CK3: MAUP Analysis

## Results

We compared changes in net jobs (WAC − RAC) across nine Bay Area counties from 2017 to 2023 at three geographic scales: county, city, and census tract.

The median absolute change was 17,478 jobs at the county level, 780 jobs at the city level, and 170 jobs at the tract level.

The total net-job change was +101,195 at both county and tract levels. The city-level total was +97,470, possibly because the city data does not cover all unincorporated areas. This difference needs further verification.

## MAUP and Ecological Fallacy

The Modifiable Areal Unit Problem (MAUP) means that results can look different when we change geographic boundaries or scales.

County-level results show broader regional changes, but they can hide differences between neighborhoods. Tract-level results show more local variation.

We use all three scales because each answers a different question. A limitation is that larger areas naturally have larger absolute job changes, so these values are not directly comparable measures of impact.

We must also avoid the ecological fallacy. A tract gaining jobs does not mean that every resident living there has more employment opportunities.

Future analysis could compare normalized job changes and examine whether the patterns remain similar across geographic scales.

**Source:** LEHD LODES8 WAC/RAC, 2017–2023.
